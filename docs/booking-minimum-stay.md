# Beds24 minimum-stay rules: a reminder

## The key rule: “Stay Through”

**The total booking length must satisfy the minimum-stay requirement for every occupied night—not just the arrival date.**

For a stay to qualify, its number of nights must be at least the **highest minimum** among the nights it occupies.

This does **not** mean adding the minimums together, or staying seven additional nights after reaching a restricted date.

## Checkout day does not count as an occupied night

A booking from **24 to 27 December** occupies the nights of **24, 25 and 26 December**. The minimum-stay setting on 27 December does not apply to that stay.

A separate **No Check-out** restriction on 27 December would still prevent departure that day.

## Example

Assume the calendar requires:

- **24–26 December:** minimum 3 nights.
- **27–31 December:** minimum 7 nights.

| Booking | Nights | Meets the minimum-stay rule? |
|---|---:|---|
| 24–27 December | 3 | Yes: all occupied nights require only 3. |
| 26–29 December | 3 | No: the stay includes nights requiring 7. |
| 27–30 December | 3 | No: the occupied nights require 7. |
| 28–31 December | 3 | No: the occupied nights require 7. |
| 25 December–1 January | 7 | Yes, provided 25 December also has a minimum no higher than 7. |

These are examples, not a live availability report. Room stock, maximum stays, arrival/departure restrictions and rate-plan conditions must also allow the booking.

## Why the nearest-date search may suggest an earlier date

The search keeps the requested number of nights and looks for the closest valid stay. For a three-night request, it must skip any stay overlapping a seven-night minimum under “Stay Through”.

In the example above, **24–27 December** can qualify while **27–30 December** cannot.

## Beds24 also supports “Arrival”

“Stay Through” is configurable, not a universal rule.

- **Stay Through:** check the minimum for every occupied night.
- **Arrival / First Night:** check the date-specific minimum on the arrival date.

The room’s restriction strategy is configured in **Beds24 → Settings → Properties → Rooms → Setup**. Room-level limits and rate-plan conditions still apply.

Do not change to “Arrival” just to produce earlier date suggestions: that changes which bookings are allowed.
