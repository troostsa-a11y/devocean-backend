Getting to Ponta do Ouro from Kosi Bay and Maputo

Via the Kosi Bay border crossing, Ponta do Ouro village is just 13 km and about 20 minutes away. From Maputo, it's roughly 120 km and about two hours by road. Here is everything you need to know to arrive smoothly.

The Two Main Routes

Most visitors arrive via one of two routes: from South Africa via the Kosi Bay border crossing, or from Maputo by road. Both are practical; the Kosi Bay route is shorter and the most common for guests arriving from southern Africa and overland travellers.

Most popular route

Via Kosi Bay Border

13 km · ~20 minutes from border

The Kosi Bay / Ponta do Ouro border crossing is the closest land border to the village. From the South African side at Kosi Bay, the village is 13 km — a straightforward drive on an improving road.

From Durban: approximately 4 hours to the border. From Johannesburg: approximately 6 hours to the border via the N2. From iSimangaliso Wetland Park: approximately 45 minutes.

From Mozambique

Via Maputo & Katembe Bridge

~120 km · ~2 hours by road

From Maputo city or Maputo International Airport (MPM), cross the bay on the Maputo–Katembe Bridge, then head south on the paved EN1 coast road straight to Ponta do Ouro — approximately 120 km in total.

Flying into Maputo is the most practical option for international travellers. Several shuttle and transfer services connect Maputo airport to Ponta do Ouro via this route.

From South Africa via the Kosi Bay Border

The Kosi Bay border crossing — formally the Ponta do Ouro / Kosi Bay Land Port of Entry — sits at the end of the tarred road through iSimangaliso Wetland Park in northern KwaZulu-Natal. The South African side is inside the park; allow time for the park gate fee if entering from the iSimangaliso direction.

1

Approach the border (South African side)

Clear South African immigration and customs. Have your passport, vehicle registration papers and proof of vehicle insurance for Mozambique. Most rental car companies now permit travel to Mozambique with advance notice — confirm before travel.

2

Mozambique immigration and visa

Entry requirements depend on your passport and purpose of travel. Mozambique offers an online eVisa as well as visa-on-arrival at some land borders. Check the official Mozambique eVisa portal for the rule that applies to your nationality — policies change and we cannot guarantee any exemption list is current. Have USD or Metical cash for any border fees; card payment is not always available.

3

Drive to the village — 13 km

From the border, Ponta do Ouro village is 13 km. The road has been improved in recent years and is navigable by standard vehicles. Some sandy patches remain. DEVOCEAN Lodge is in the village centre — ask at the border gate or use What3words: ///slick.jars.rates.

Border hours: The Kosi Bay / Ponta do Ouro land border is listed by the South African Border Management Authority as open 08:00–17:00 daily (last checked July 2026 — verify current hours at the official SARBMA ports-of-entry page; hours can be extended on public holidays). Peak queues occur on South African and Mozambican public holidays and during the December–January peak season. Arriving before 10:00 or between 14:00–15:30 typically means shorter waits. Plan to clear the border well before 17:00.

From Maputo by Road or Transfer

Maputo International Airport (MPM) has direct flights from Johannesburg, Nairobi, Dar es Salaam, Addis Ababa and several regional hubs. From the airport, Ponta do Ouro is roughly 120 km south — around 2 hours in good traffic.

The standard route from Maputo crosses the bay on the Maputo–Katembe Bridge (opened 2018) and then continues south on the paved EN1 coast road directly to Ponta do Ouro. The road is tar all the way to the village — no 4×4 required. Note that some sections near the coast can be slow after heavy rain.

Self-drive from Maputo

  • Cross the Maputo–Katembe Bridge heading south
  • Follow the EN1 coast road south to Ponta do Ouro
  • Total: ~120 km, allow 2–2.5 hours

Public Transport — Chapas

If you are travelling without a vehicle, chapas (shared minibus taxis) provide the most common form of public transport in Mozambique. They run regularly between the Kosi Bay border and Ponta do Ouro village throughout the day, departing when full and taking approximately 20–30 minutes.

From Maputo, chapas and intercity minibuses run south towards the border area, with connections to Ponta do Ouro. The journey from Maputo by public transport takes longer than by private vehicle and involves at least one change — but it is perfectly feasible for independent travellers.

DEVOCEAN Lodge is 150 metres from the village transport terminal — the central point where chapas arrive and depart. If you arrive by public transport, the lodge is a very short walk.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Do I need a visa to enter Mozambique at the Kosi Bay border?
    Entry requirements depend on your nationality and purpose of travel. Mozambique offers an online eVisa as well as visa-on-arrival at some crossings. Visa rules change — do not rely on informal exemption lists. Check the official Mozambique eVisa portal for the rule that applies to your passport before you travel. Have USD or Metical cash for any border fees.
  • What are the border opening hours?
    The South African Border Management Authority lists Kosi Bay as open 08:00–17:00 daily (last checked July 2026). Hours can be extended on public holidays. Always verify current hours at the official SARBMA page before travel. Peak queues on South African and Mozambican public holidays and December–January — plan to clear the border well before 17:00.
  • Can I take a normal car (not a 4×4) to Ponta do Ouro?
    Yes. The main route from the Kosi Bay border to the village centre is navigable by standard vehicles. DEVOCEAN Lodge is on a navigable road. See our full guide: Visiting Ponta do Ouro Without a 4×4.
  • Is there public transport from the border?
    Yes — shared chapas run between the border and the village throughout the day, departing when full. The journey is about 20–30 minutes. DEVOCEAN Lodge is 150 m from the village transport terminal.
  • Can DEVOCEAN Lodge arrange a transfer?
    Yes. We arrange transfers from the Kosi Bay border (~20 min) and from Maputo city or airport (~2 hours). Contact us in advance or ask Marin when you book.
  • Can I take a rental car from South Africa into Mozambique?
    Many rental companies now permit Mozambique travel with advance notice and a cross-border letter. Confirm with your rental company before booking. Some companies restrict standard vehicles to the tar road, so clarify if they allow the coastal route.
