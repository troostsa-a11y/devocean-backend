Where We Are

DEVOCEAN Lodge is set in a lush tropical garden in the heart of Ponta do Ouro village, Southern Mozambique. The Indian Ocean is a few minutes' walk away through the village streets. The main beach — wide, uncrowded and framed by casuarina trees — is reached on foot without needing a vehicle.

The village centre, with its restaurants, bars and dive operators, is immediately accessible. The public transport terminal — where shared chapas depart for the Kosi Bay border and Maputo — is just 150 metres from the gate. Ponta do Ouro is a genuinely walkable destination, and DEVOCEAN Lodge is positioned at its centre.

The approach road to Ponta do Ouro is tarred and accessible by standard car. A 4×4 is not required to reach the lodge. Ample on-site parking is available for guests who drive.

Activities From Your Doorstep

Ponta do Ouro is one of Southern Africa's most rewarding adventure destinations — and almost everything is within easy reach of the lodge on foot or by a short local transfer.

In the water

  • Scuba diving — multiple PADI operators nearby
  • Ethical wild dolphin swims (year-round)
  • Whale watching (June – November)
  • Ocean seafaris (whale shark, turtles, rays)
  • Snorkelling, surfing, deep-sea fishing

On land

  • Beach walks and sunrise swims
  • Maputo National Park day trips — 30 km
  • Village restaurants, bars and craft stalls
  • Kosi Bay iSimangaliso Wetlands — 25 min
  • Cross-border bush and cultural trips

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How far is DEVOCEAN Lodge from the beach?
    A few minutes' walk. The lodge is in the village centre, and Ponta do Ouro's main beach is easily reached on foot. No vehicle is needed for the beach.
  • Is a 4×4 needed to reach the lodge?
    No. The main road from the Kosi Bay border to Ponta do Ouro village is tarred, and the lodge is on a navigable road. A standard car is perfectly fine. See our full guide: Visiting Ponta do Ouro Without a 4×4.
  • Is DEVOCEAN Lodge good for families?
    Yes. The Garden Cottage and Thatched Chalet each accommodate two adults and one child. The environment is safe and relaxed. Dolphin boat trips are popular with children and a highlight for most young guests.
  • What is the best way to get to Ponta do Ouro?
    Most guests arrive via the Kosi Bay border crossing from South Africa — just 13 km from the village. Others fly into Maputo and drive or transfer (~120 km, about 2 hours). See our full guide: Getting to Ponta do Ouro.
  • What about meals — is breakfast included?
    Yes — breakfast is included in the accommodation rate, served daily in the garden (08:30–11:00). Resident guests can also pre-order dinner from our in-house kitchen (latest order 20:00). We do not serve lunch, but there are over 16 restaurants and cafés within a short walk. Outdoor braai facilities are also available. See our full guide: Food at DEVOCEAN Lodge.
  • Can I book directly and get the best rate?
    Yes — book direct at devoceanlodge.com/book-direct for live availability and guaranteed best rates. Booking is confirmed immediately with no OTA commission added.
