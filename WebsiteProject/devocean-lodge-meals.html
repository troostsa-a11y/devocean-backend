Breakfast in the Garden Included

Breakfast is included in your accommodation rate and is normally served between 08:30 and 11:00 in the tropical garden.

Guests can choose from our breakfast menu, with both cooked and lighter options. Fresh Portuguese bread is served daily, accompanied by coffee, tea or hot chocolate.

Early dive or dolphin swim?

If you have an early departure, dive trip, dolphin swim or other morning activity, please speak with us beforehand. An earlier or later breakfast can usually be arranged.

Dinner at DEVOCEAN Pre-order

Our in-house kitchen prepares dinner exclusively for guests staying at the lodge. Meals are prepared to order using ingredients purchased fresh each day, so the available choices may vary.

Please order in advance — preferably earlier in the day. The latest time for placing a dinner order is 20:00, and the kitchen closes at 21:00.

Because we cook specifically for the guests who order, we can concentrate on freshness and quality while avoiding unnecessary food waste.

How to order

  • Let us know your dinner choice during the day
  • Latest order time: 20:00
  • Kitchen closes: 21:00
  • Choices may vary with daily market availability
  • Prepared fresh to order — no set menu

Resident guests only

Dinner service is reserved for guests staying at DEVOCEAN Lodge. We do not offer the kitchen to outside visitors. This lets us focus entirely on freshness and quality for the guests in our care.

Dietary Requirements

Please tell us about any vegetarian, vegan or other dietary requirements before arrival, or as early as possible during your stay. Our kitchen is small and local supplies vary, but we will always explain honestly what we can accommodate.

The earlier we know, the better we can plan around what is available locally on the day.

Lunch

DEVOCEAN Lodge does not operate a regular lunch service. Ponta do Ouro has numerous cafés, beach bars and restaurants within walking distance of the lodge. We are happy to suggest places based on what is currently open and what you would like to eat — just ask us or Marin.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Is breakfast included?
    Yes. Breakfast is included in the accommodation rate for all stays.
  • What time is breakfast served?
    Breakfast is normally served from 08:30 until 11:00. Earlier or later service can often be arranged when requested beforehand — for example, for an early dive or dolphin swim departure.
  • Can I have dinner at the lodge?
    Yes. Resident guests can order dinner from our in-house kitchen. Please order in advance and no later than 20:00. The kitchen closes at 21:00.
  • Is the kitchen open to outside visitors?
    No. Our meal service is reserved for guests staying at DEVOCEAN Lodge.
  • Do you serve lunch?
    We do not offer regular lunch service. We are happy to suggest nearby cafés and restaurants based on what is currently open and what you would like to eat.
  • Can you accommodate dietary requirements?
    Often, yes. Please advise us beforehand so that we can confirm what can be prepared with the ingredients available locally. Our kitchen is small and local supplies vary, but we will always be honest about what we can accommodate.
