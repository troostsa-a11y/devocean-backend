---
name: Guide editorial sequencing
description: Finalize English guide and experience copy before translations and localized SEO.
---
Update and approve the English root guide and experience text before undertaking
translations or completing their localized SEO metadata. Fully translated guides
remain the intended end state.
The hold covers guide/experience body copy and SEO, not short navigation labels
or first-render UI language fixes.

**Why:** The owner explicitly wants to avoid translating outdated source text and
then repeating the translation work after editorial corrections.
The owner separately approved fixing UI language fallbacks and translating
navigation labels while the main-text and SEO work remains deferred.

**How to apply:** Do not treat an indexing report as authorization to translate
unfinished guides or consolidate their locale URLs. Address independently
confirmed routing bugs separately; await an explicit content-update request.
Leave localized titles, descriptions and social-sharing metadata unchanged until
the main text is final.
UI-only language fixes may proceed using existing approved translations; short
navigation labels may be translated without reopening the deferred body/SEO work.
