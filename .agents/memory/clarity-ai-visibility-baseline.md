## Two content gaps to close

DEVOCEAN has `/ponta-do-ouro-without-4x4` and `/getting-to-ponta-do-ouro` but they lack structured transport data (routes, durations, border crossing hours, operators, costs).  
**Fix:** Add tables / structured lists of practical transport info to both guide pages.

### 2. Seasonal travel planning

DEVOCEAN has no dedicated "best time to visit" page.  
**Fix:** Create a page covering dry/wet season, water visibility calendar, whale/dolphin season, school-holiday pricing.
