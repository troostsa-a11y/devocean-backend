## Rule

Keep domestic transport to Maputo National Park separate from the Durban/Kosi Bay cross-border journey. Guests may call the park "Maputo Nature Reserve"; this does not make the South African border route relevant.

**Why:** Marin incorrectly sent a guest from Maputo towards Kosi Bay and told them to cross a border. The user supplied the correct local directions.

**How to apply:** Use the lodge-confirmed chapa departure points: Maputo's Old Fort near the fishing harbour, or the parking-area exit opposite Ponta do Ouro's local market. The conductor should be asked for a roadside drop-off near the Park Headquarters or Main Entrance, not a border transfer.

Always accompany these directions with the park's 4x4-only access, the absence of accommodation near the entrance gate, and the need for a confirmed park game-drive reservation before travelling this way.

**Why:** A chapa drop-off is not access to a game drive, and arriving unprepared can leave a guest without onward transport or nearby accommodation.
